One image summing up Tuesday 24 November 2026 in world news: a cream
broadsheet front page with 48 stories — three leads (Nvidia reinstating
Jensen Huang as CEO and Wall Street posting its best session since April
2025, NATO vowing to defend space after reports Russia may launch a
nuclear-armed satellite within 60 days, three oil tankers catching fire
after hitting mines in the Strait of Hormuz), twelve illustrated tiles
(Bitcoin spiking to $64,446 then sinking back below $60,000, Russia
warning Estonia it is "on verge of triggering open war", a Russian strike
killing 2 and wounding 22 in Zaporizhzhia, fourteen nations backing the
ICJ's Myanmar genocide ruling, a US drone helping catch "El Chapo Isidro",
a M5.3 quake shaking Nepal, Sanofi buying Insmed for $41bn in cash,
strikes killing three in Gaza including a UNRWA worker, Brentford
thrashing 10-man Everton 4-0, Russian jets entering South Korea's air
zone, the Pentagon rebuking Canada over defence, the US losing its measles
elimination status), a dotted world map with 15 numbered pins, a UTC
timeline, BY FIELD / BY REGION count strips, and a 33-item "Also" list.
Every headline and figure comes from the day's reporting (Reuters, AP,
CoinDesk, Bloomberg, Al Jazeera, BBC, NCS, FT, Daily Summary, Principia,
Horizon, 10Things); disputed claims are attributed, not stated as fact,
and a DATE CHECK box names the earlier stories deliberately left out —
including yesterday's COP31 collapse, the Merkury sinking, and the Sparta
II tow. The image is rendered deterministically with Python PIL and
contains no AI-generated art.
